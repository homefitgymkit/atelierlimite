What are Artist Editions by Atelier Limité?

Atelier Limité
Sydney, NSW
10 min read

An Atelier Limité Artist Edition is a fixed, numbered run of garments made in collaboration with a single artist. Every piece carries a unique number, the artist's name on the label, and a certificate of edition. When the run is closed, the number on every certificate is permanent. No more will ever exist.

The anatomy of an edition

Every Atelier Limité edition has five defining components. Together, they make an edition what it is, and distinguish it categorically from a conventional clothing release.

How an edition number works

The edition number is the most specific thing on the garment. It is not a batch code or a manufacturing reference, it is the individual piece's identity within the edition, and its relationship to every other piece that will ever exist.

An edition vs. a drop, what's the difference?

The language matters. A drop is a marketing event: a timed release designed to generate urgency, create a moment, and drive a spike in transactions. The mechanics of a drop, limited time windows, countdown clocks, artificial stock restrictions, are designed to manufacture the feeling of scarcity rather than the reality of it.

The quarterly cadence, four editions a year

Atelier Limité releases four editions per year, one per quarter. Each edition is distinct: a new artist, a new body of work, a new run. The cadence keeps the programme alive without diluting what each edition means.

What garments are in an edition, and how they're made

Each edition draws from four garment types, all produced on GOTS-certified organic cotton carrying Fair Wear Foundation accreditation. The specific selection varies by edition. All pieces are available in the brand's three colourways: studio black, atelier white, and raw canvas.

What every collector receives

The package that arrives with an Atelier Limité edition is designed to open the way a gallery acquisition does, considered, material, unhurried. The unboxing is not a content moment. It is the final act of the edition, and the first moment the collector holds something that has a permanent number.
