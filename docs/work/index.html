What we look for

Merit

A compelling artistic practice and work with strong visual conviction. Not a follower count, the work itself.

Translation

Work that can carry meaningfully onto a wearable surface. Any discipline: paint, sculpture, photography, print, music, more.

Story

A practice with a point of view. We are explicitly looking in places the traditional gallery system has not.

Values

Artists who want a fair, transparent, lasting relationship, not a one-off flat-fee licence.

How a collaboration works

From a first email to a quarterly cheque.

01

You reach out

Email artists@atelierlimite.com with your practice and a sample of work. Every submission gets a genuine response within two weeks.

02

We talk

If there's a fit, we discuss the edition: the work, the theme, the timeline. Nothing is committed until you're ready.

03

You deliver artwork

Print-ready files, eight weeks before the opening. You approve the final garment sample before anything is produced.

04

The edition opens

We manufacture, market, fulfil, and administer. You promote to your audience if you like. Then you're paid 50%, quarterly.

Our commitments

The terms are the same for everyone, by design.

·No upfront cost, no financial risk if an edition underperforms.
·Full IP retained, the licence covers one edition only.
·A transparent quarterly statement showing every sale and cost.
·No minimum following, no gallery history, no geographic limit.
