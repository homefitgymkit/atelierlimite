How it works

01

Artist selected

Each edition is themed. We find artists whose work deserves a wider audience, painters, sculptors, photographers, musicians, and more.

02

Edition produced

Limited run, numbered. Screen-printed on GOTS-certified organic cotton with water-based inks. The artist's name on every label.

03

You acquire

Your piece arrives with a 350gsm certificate of edition, an artist postcard, a mini poster, and a wax-sealed package.

04

The artist gets paid

50% of net profit goes directly to the artist, every quarter, with a full transparent statement. The artist keeps their IP.
