Common questions

Everything about how Atelier Limité works.

How does the 50/50 artist split work?

We split net profit equally with every artist — calculated after direct costs (blanks, printing, packaging, fulfilment), not from gross revenue. Artists retain their IP and are paid quarterly with a transparent statement.

What comes in the package?

A matte kraft box, branded tissue, natural wax seal, 350gsm certificate of edition with your unique number, artist postcard, artist statement, mini poster, care card, and a next edition preview. No virgin plastic.

Are editions ever restocked?

Never. Each edition consists of a fixed number of pieces. Once closed, it's closed permanently. This is fundamental to the numbered-edition model and the value of each piece's certificate.

How can I collaborate as an artist?

Email artists@atelierlimite.com. We consider all disciplines — painters, sculptors, jewellers, musicians, photographers, and more. No minimum following. The only criteria is a compelling practice and work that translates meaningfully to a wearable surface.

About Atelier Limité

Atelier Limité is a limited edition artist apparel brand founded in Sydney, Australia in 2025. The brand produces capsule collections in collaboration with emerging artists, with each piece numbered, named, and certified. The company model involves a 50/50 net profit split with every collaborating artist. Artists retain intellectual property rights. The brand is pursuing B Corp certification and plants one tree per piece sold.

Brand values and commitments

  • 50% of net profit paid directly to collaborating artists, quarterly
  • Artists retain IP — Atelier Limité licenses artwork only
  • One tree planted per piece sold through One Tree Planted or Greenfleet
  • No virgin plastic in packaging
  • GOTS-certified organic cotton blanks
  • Water-based and discharge inks only
  • Carbon-neutral domestic shipping via Sendle
  • B Corp certification in progress
  • Limited production — no overruns, no surplus stock
